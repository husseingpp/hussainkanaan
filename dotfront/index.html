DOTFRONT

Lasso your dots, draw their path, cut off the enemy's cities, and watch their army starve.

How to play · 60 seconds

  • Select — drag a loop around your blue dots.
  • Move — click to send them; drag a curved path to flank.
  • Build — click your city to make Light / Heavy units.
  • Win — take the enemy capital and most of the cities. Surround their army and it starves.
