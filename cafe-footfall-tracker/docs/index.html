Which neighbourhood does coffee best?

Achrafieh comes out as the strongest all-round zone, combining a 4.33 average rating with the healthiest review sentiment. Rating and sentiment mostly move together, but the gaps between them show where star ratings flatter (or undersell) the actual customer experience.

Peak hours across the week

Café availability peaks around Monday 10:00 in Achrafieh, when 6 spots are open at once. The bright mid-day band shows Beirut's café culture is an all-week, late-into-the-evening affair rather than a weekend phenomenon.

Rating vs popularity

Popularity and quality are only loosely linked: Starbucks Verdun draws the most reviews (1471) without topping the ratings. Watch the lower-right corner — heavily reviewed but modestly rated cafés trade on location, not loyalty.

Sentiment vs star ratings

Sentiment climbs almost monotonically with star rating, which is a good sanity check on the multilingual scoring (VADER for English, a custom lexicon for Arabic). Where a bucket breaks the trend, reviewers are saying one thing and clicking another — those gaps feed the contradiction analysis.

Cafés trailing their neighbourhood

2 cafés sit more than half a star below the neighbourhood average, with Costa Hamra (Hamra) furthest adrift at -0.77. Cross-checking against review sentiment separates genuinely weak operators from places with a few loud detractors.
