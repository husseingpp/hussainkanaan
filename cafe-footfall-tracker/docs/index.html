Which neighbourhood does coffee best?

Hamra comes out as the strongest all-round zone, combining a 4.46 average rating with the healthiest review sentiment. Rating and sentiment mostly move together, but the gaps between them show where star ratings flatter (or undersell) the actual customer experience.

Peak hours across the week

Café availability peaks around Monday 00:00 in Antelias, when 8 spots are open at once. The bright mid-day band shows Beirut's café culture is an all-week, late-into-the-evening affair rather than a weekend phenomenon.

Rating vs popularity

Popularity and quality are only loosely linked: السنيور draws the most reviews (2527) without topping the ratings. Watch the lower-right corner — heavily reviewed but modestly rated cafés trade on location, not loyalty.

Sentiment vs star ratings

No scored review texts in the current dataset — Excel scraper exports carry review counts but not review bodies, so sentiment charts populate only when data comes from the Places API (or another source with text).

Cafés trailing their neighbourhood

23 cafés sit more than half a star below the neighbourhood average, with 3arouset Lebneh (Hamra) furthest adrift at -2.16. Cross-checking against review sentiment separates genuinely weak operators from places with a few loud detractors.
